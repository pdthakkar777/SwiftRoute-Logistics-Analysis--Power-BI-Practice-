# SwiftRoute-Logistics-Analysis--Power-BI-Practice-
End to end logistics and operational performance analysis using Power BI focusing on delivery efficiency, hub performance, driver and vehicle productivity etc. 
## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the operational performance of SwiftRoute Logistics using Microsoft Power BI. The analysis evaluates delivery efficiency, customer satisfaction, hub performance, driver productivity and vehicle reliability to support data-driven business decisions.

The interactive dashboard enables stakeholders to identify operational bottlenecks, monitor key performance indicators and explore opportunities for improving logistics efficiency.
## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall logistics and delivery performance.
- Evaluate on-time delivery rates and customer satisfaction.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming logistics hubs.
- Analyze driver performance, experience and delivery delays.
- Evaluate fleet availability and vehicle breakdown patterns.
- Identify seasonal trends in order volumes.
- Support operational planning and resource allocation.
## Dataset Description

The project consists of four interconnected datasets:

| Dataset | Records | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Orders | 27,979 | Order details, delivery status, customer satisfaction and processing time |
| Drivers | 55 | Driver experience, employment details and performance ratings |
| Hubs | 6 | Logistics hubs and their operational capacities |
| Vehicles | 45 | Vehicle models, status, breakdowns and maintenance information |

The analysis covers logistics operations across 2023 and 2024.

## Key Business Insights

### Overall Business Performance – December 2024

- Total orders reached 1,224, representing 11.2% month-over-month growth.
- On-time delivery rate improved from 77.3% to 79.9%.
- Customer satisfaction increased from 82.2% to 84.9%.
- Average delivery time decreased by approximately 3.9%, reaching 34.76 hours.

### Hub Performance

- El Paso Hub demonstrated consistent performance across 2023 and 2024.
- El Paso Hub achieved an on-time delivery rate of 84.4% in December 2024.
- Saturday recorded the highest average order processing time of 39.45 hours in December 2024.
- Dallas Main Hub processed more orders than its displayed capacity in December 2024, indicating a potential capacity constraint.

### Driver Performance

- May, October and December were among the busiest months in 2024.
- Richard Brown recorded the highest displayed delivery delay percentage of 42.86% in December 2024.
- Driver-level performance monitoring can support targeted training and workload optimization.

### Fleet Performance

- 33 out of 45 vehicles were active, representing 73.33% of the fleet.
- Vans contributed 62.34% of total orders in December 2024.
- Freightliner M2 and Mercedes Sprinter were leading contributors to delivered orders by vehicle model.
- Higher breakdown counts were observed among vehicles older than six years, highlighting the need for preventive maintenance analysis.
